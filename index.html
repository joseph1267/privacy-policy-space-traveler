Privacy Policy for Space Travelers

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Developer: AI Developer · App: Space Travelers (com.aideveloper.spacetravelers)

This policy explains what information the Android game Space Travelers ("the game") handles, and why. "We" and "us" mean AI Developer, the developer of the game.

In short: we don't collect, store, sell or share any of your personal information. We have no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Some optional features are provided by Google: purchases, cloud saves, achievements and backups. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

1. Information we collect

None. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, location or contacts. It doesn't send us any information, and we have no way to see who plays it or how.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game keeps files in its private storage on your device. Other apps can't read this storage. The files are:

  • Save files: your ship, inventory, pilot level, map exploration, quests and settings.
  • Medal progress: which in-game medals you've earned.
  • Purchase records: the names of store items you bought and their Google Play purchase tokens, so the game delivers each purchase exactly once.

These files stay on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

3. Services provided by Google

The following features are provided by Google. When you use them, Google processes the related data under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service. We don't receive this data.

FeatureWhat happensIs it optional?
In-app purchases (Google Play Billing) Google handles payment and billing details. The game only learns which items you own, so it can deliver them. We never see your payment information. Yes. The game is free to play, and nothing in it requires a purchase.
Cloud save (Google Play Games, Saved Games) If you sign in to Google Play Games, copies of your save files are stored in your Google account. Upload happens when you choose BACK UP and when you leave the game. Download happens only when you choose RESTORE. Yes. Only if you sign in.
Achievements (Google Play Games) If you're signed in, medals you earn and your progress towards them are recorded in your Google Play Games profile. Google may show them there, alongside your Play Games gamer name and avatar. Yes. Only if you sign in.
Android backup If backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the game's save files in your device backup to your Google account. They're restored when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. Yes. It's controlled in your device settings.

The Google Play Games software in the game may also send Google anonymous diagnostics and crash information to keep its services working. See Google's Play Games Services data disclosure for details.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by Google Play services for purchases, cloud saves and achievements. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): used only for in-app purchases.

The game doesn't request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

5. Advertising and analytics

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics tools. Nothing is used to track you across apps or websites.

6. Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Space Travelers → Storage → Clear data.
  • Cloud saves and achievements: delete them from your Google Play Games profile or your Google Account. You can also simply not sign in to Google Play Games.
  • Purchase history: it's kept by Google Play and managed in your Google account.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to delete. If you have questions, contact us (section 9).

7. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
